📊 What do these stats mean?

Total (kWh): Total energy consumed over the visible time range. Calculated by integrating power over time.

Avg (W): Average power consumption across all visible devices and time points in the selected range.

Mean (W): Statistical mean (average) of all power readings in the visible time range.

Median (W): Middle value when all power readings are sorted - less affected by spikes/outliers than mean.

💡 Tip: These stats update based on your selected time range. Zoom/pan the chart to see stats for specific periods!
